# Bar panels

Date: 2026-10-15 07:19 EDT

## Context

Each indicator on the top bar opens a panel below it with that indicator's controls, so the common settings are a
click away: Wi-Fi on and off and joining a network (with a password field for secured ones), Bluetooth power,
scanning and connecting devices, volume, mute and the output device, screen brightness, battery state with power
actions, the weather forecast and a calendar. Tray items show their own menus. A panel closes on a click outside it,
on Escape and on a second click of its icon; one panel is open at a time. The bar talks to the system's own services
(NetworkManager, BlueZ, PipeWire, UPower, the backlight) rather than launching terminal programs. What the bar is and
how it starts is in [shell/bar.md](../intents/shell/bar.md).

## Panels

- [x] A shared panel: anchored under its icon, inside the output, closes on outside click, Escape or the icon; one open at a time
- [ ] Network: Wi-Fi switch, the visible networks with signal and lock, connect (password field for secured networks), disconnect, the connection in use
- [ ] Bluetooth: power switch, scan, paired and found devices, connect, disconnect, pair
- [x] Audio: volume slider, mute, the output device to use
- [x] Brightness: a slider for the backlight; the icon stays hidden without one
- [x] Power: battery level, state and time left, power profile where the system offers one, suspend, restart, power off, log out
- [x] Weather: the forecast for the coming days
- [x] Clock: a month calendar with today marked
- [x] Tray: an item's menu on right click
