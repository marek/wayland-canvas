# Touch and the pen

Date: 2026-10-19 12:03 EDT

## Context

The laptop's touchscreen and its stylus work in every window and on the
compositor's own UI; today the compositor takes neither device.

- A touch on a window reaches its app as touch. The pen reaches it as a
  tablet tool with pressure and tilt, or as the pointer when the app has no
  tablet support. Krita, which runs through Xwayland, gets both through X.
- On the compositor's own UI (title bars and their buttons, resize edges,
  the overview, the shelf, the launcher, the capture UI) a finger and the
  pen act as the mouse's left button. A finger held still is a right click,
  as press-and-hold is on Windows; the pen's barrel button is the right
  button.
- Two fingers move the view: a two-finger drag looks away, as Super and a
  scroll do, and a pinch does what Super and a two-finger pinch do on the
  touchpad. One finger on empty canvas moves nothing, as a mouse drag
  without Super does.
- Three- and four-finger swipes on the touchscreen do what the touchpad's
  do, and the gesture preset reassigns both alike.
- A locked session gives touch and the pen to the locker only.
- A press, touch or pen tip meant for an X11 window reaches X only once X
  has taken in every window move sent before it, so none lands by X's old
  picture; a stalled X holds input no longer than a compile-time limit.
- The touchscreen and the pen map to the panel they are built into.

Out of scope: an on-screen keyboard, the button pads of external tablets,
and gestures made with the pen.
